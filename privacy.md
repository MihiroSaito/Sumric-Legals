# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 14, 2026**

Sumric (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects the privacy of users and is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the following policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

* Device ID (used as an identifier for anonymous login)
* Metadata related to content (e.g., summary results or processing information)
* App usage information (e.g., number of launches, operation logs)

In addition, information such as article URLs or text submitted by users to the Service may be temporarily processed on our servers for the purpose of generating summaries.

---

## 2. Purpose of Use

The collected information is used for the following purposes:

* Providing and improving the Service
* Providing article summarization and related features
* Troubleshooting and user support

---

## 3. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties except in the following cases:

* When required by law
* When the user has given consent

However, we may use external services such as cloud services or AI services in order to provide the functionality of the Service.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

User data will be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the Service.
Users may delete stored content or history through features within the app or by contacting support.

---

## 5. User Rights

Users have the following rights:

* Access to their information
* Request correction or deletion of their information
* Data export (planned)

These rights will be honored within a reasonable scope unless restricted by applicable law.

---

## 6. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect collected information.

Examples include:

* Encrypted communication
* Access control
* Management of cloud infrastructure for data protection

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy without prior notice.
If significant changes are made, we will notify users through appropriate methods such as in-app notifications.
