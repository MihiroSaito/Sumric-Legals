Privacy Policy (Sumric)

Last Updated: March 25, 2026

Sumric (the "Company", "we", "our", or "us") establishes this Privacy Policy (the "Policy") regarding the handling of user information in the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information.

(1) Information Provided by Users

  • Account information (such as login information)
  • Information such as URLs entered or submitted by users

(2) Information Collected Through Service Use

  • Article metadata such as URL, title, and thumbnail URL
  • Article body data
  • Summary results
  • Usage history (operation history such as summary execution, viewing, and sharing)

(3) Technical Information

  • Device information
  • App usage status
  • Notification tokens (for push notifications)

2. Purpose of Use

We use collected information for the following purposes.

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To provide article summarization and search features
  • To improve user convenience
  • To prevent and respond to unauthorized use
  • To send notices regarding the Service

3. Use of AI Services

  1. We use AI technologies for processing such as article summarization.
  2. Article metadata and body text may be sent to external AI services.
  3. We handle data in accordance with the terms and conditions of such external services.

4. Data Management

  1. Article content may be managed as shared data to improve service efficiency.
  2. Users' usage history and usage status are managed on a per-user basis.

5. Scope of Data Use

  1. We use collected data to improve user convenience and the Service.
  2. We do not use stored article body data to train our own AI models.

6. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide users' personal information to third parties except as required by law.

However, the following case is excluded.

  • When using external services (such as AI services) within the scope necessary for providing the Service

7. Data Retention Period and Deletion

  1. If a user deletes their account, data related to that user will be deleted.
  2. However, some data may remain for a certain period due to backups and similar reasons.

8. Security

We implement appropriate security control measures to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of information.

9. User Rights

Users may confirm, correct, or delete their own information through methods specified by the Company.

10. Use by Minors

Minors shall use the Service with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

11. Changes to the Policy

We may change this Policy as necessary. The revised Policy becomes effective when displayed in the Service or notified to users.

12. Contact

For inquiries regarding this Policy, please contact us through the designated method within the Service.
